**The AI Solopreneur OS (this repo) is free — it runs as plain text in Claude Code.
W_Counsel is the packaged, paid version: the same OS with a face, a voice, and a
15-minute onboarding that sets it up for you.**

It's the easy button for people who'd rather *talk* to their system than type
commands in a terminal. You speak (mic), it reasons against your own data folder, and
a 3D 军师 (诸葛 / Zhuge Liang) answers back in 中文 or English — running on the Claude
login you already have, your knowledge never leaving your machine.

- **It has a face.** A desktop strategist that listens, thinks, and speaks back — not a chat box in a costume.
- **It runs on you, not a vendor.** Your Claude sign-in or your own `sk-ant-…` key. Your data stays on your machine.
- **It onboards you.** A 15-minute pixel-kingdom configures the OS to *you* — answer the six halls and it's tuned.
- **It compounds.** Say `入库` / "ingest" and it files what you feed it into your **库 Arsenal**.
- **It stays in its lane.** It may edit *only* its own data folder; everything else on your disk is read-only.

The free OS — the framework and the `/commands` — lives in this repo and runs in Claude
Code today. **W_Counsel is the upgrade for people who'd rather not operate it themselves.**
